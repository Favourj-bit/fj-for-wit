01 · About

Hi, I’m FJ.

I’m a machine learning engineer and researcher from Nigeria, and I believe community is what turns potential into a career.

I’m studying for an MSc in Engineering Artificial Intelligence here at CMU-Africa. My research is about reliable, trustworthy AI: factuality, evaluation, robustness and knowledge graphs.

Before CMU-Africa, I spent a year as a Machine Learning Engineer at the Cytoscape Consortium, turning my Google Summer of Code project into textToKnowledgeGraph, a tool that converts scientific papers into knowledge graphs that biologists can explore.

None of that happened alone. IEEE, GDSC, AWS Cloud Club and Data Science Nigeria at OAU are where I learned to build, lead and speak. Now I want to give that back, especially to women in STEM, so every one of us can reach our highest potential.
